## Glance

`Sources/Island/Liveness.swift` adapts the liveness cues (glare, device bezel, nose parallax,
blink, cue evaluator) from Glance, https://github.com/jonnyoo/glance. The lock-screen unlock in
`LockScreenUnlock.swift` follows Glance's approach, and `LockFaceOverlay.swift` uses Glance's
SkyLight lock-screen window technique (itself adapted from https://github.com/Lakr233/SkyLightWindow,
MIT). The rest follows Glance's approach (type the stored password when a live,
recognised face is seen).
